$S : \mathbb{R}^{+} \to \mathbb{R}^{d}$

$t$-edik idopillanatban a keszpenz
$$
X_{t}^{0}(\phi) = z_{0} - \int_{0}^{t} \phi_{u} \cdot S_{u} \, du - \int _{0}^{t} \lambda \| \phi_{u} \| ^{\alpha} \, du,
$$
ahol $\alpha = 2$ es $\phi_{u} \cdot S_{u}$ a skalaris szorzast jeletni es $\| \cdot \|$ a kettes norma.

$$
X_{t}^{k}(\phi) = z_{k} + \int _{0}^{t} \phi_{u} \, du

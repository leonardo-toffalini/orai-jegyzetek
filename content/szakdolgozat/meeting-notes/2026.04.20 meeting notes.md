Policy network architecture
```
obs(37)
  -> Linear(37,128) + GELU
  -> LSTM(128,128)
  -> [actor: Linear(128,1), logstd param] -> Normal -> tanh+scale to [-5,5]
  -> [critic: Linear(128,1)]
```

> 	- then a single linear layer for critic head
> 	- then a single linear layer for each actor head (multiple actions)

> the idea is that the big LSTM learns everything meaningful, then the linear layers just "read out" the needed information
